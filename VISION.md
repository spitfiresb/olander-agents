# Vision

> "A week vs. two days is the entire game."

## What this is

A faster brain for Olander's inside-sales team.

Olander's reps spend their days looking things up — across catalogs, P21, vendor lists, and order history. We turn the lookup-heavy parts of that work into instant answers, so reps can spend their time on judgment, relationships, and product knowledge: the parts only humans can do.

## Why we exist

Olander doesn't win on price. When a SKU costs 22 cents, the difference between 22 and 25 is noise. ISO certification settles the quality question before the conversation starts. The only axis where Olander can actually win or lose a customer is speed — how fast they answer, how fast they ship.

The speed bottleneck isn't the warehouse. It's everything before the warehouse: the rep on the phone, on hold, flipping through five tabs and a spreadsheet. Every minute we shave off that workflow is a minute closer to the customer's "yes."

## Where this goes

1. **Now: Inside-sales lookups.** The conversations reps have dozens of times a day, made conversational and instant. Five anchor use cases:
   - **Catalog & spec lookups** — *"What size helicoil goes in this hole?"* Catalog knowledge as a query, not a search.
   - **Vendor sourcing** — *"Where do I get bronze cap screws?"* Who carries it, how fast it gets here, before the customer finishes asking.
   - **Customer order history** — *"Last 10 orders for this customer."* A sentence, not a P21 query.
   - **Marketing & account lists** — *"Who's bought stainless in the last 12 months but hasn't ordered in 90 days?"* A list, not a ticket.
   - **Inventory search** — *"All M10 1.25 screws we have."* By thread, material, length, finish, with stock on hand, immediately.
2. **Later: Every step before the warehouse.** Once the lookups are instant, the next bottleneck shows itself — quoting, sourcing, fulfillment coordination. The vision compounds: collapse the multi-day back-and-forth into a single call, then collapse the call.

Inside-sales lookups are the wedge. The shortest path from *"do you have…"* to *"on your dock Thursday"* is the destination.

## Product principles

- **Optimize for the rep's next sentence.** The right answer is the one they can read out loud to a customer without translation.
- **Augment, don't replace.** The reps' judgment, relationships, and product knowledge are the moat. We give them a faster brain, not a substitute.
- **Trust, then expand.** Start with the lookups Olander already does dozens of times a day. Earn the right to automate harder things.

## Engineering principles

- **Build against real data first.** The MVP runs on actual P21 and a real chat surface. Breadth comes after we've felt where the bottlenecks really are.
- **Latency is a bug.** A correct answer in 8 seconds loses to a correct answer in 1. Treat slowness like any other defect.
- **Defer breadth until the MVP shows what's missing.** SOW-complete is later; MVP-correct is now.

## North star metrics

- A rep answers a customer question mid-call without saying *"let me check and call you back."*
- Quotes that used to take a day go out in minutes.
- Orders that used to ship in a week ship in two days — because every step before the warehouse got faster.
- The team uses it without being told to.
